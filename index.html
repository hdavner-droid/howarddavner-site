About

From Wall Street to the beverage aisle

A short version of a long career — and where it's headed.

Howard Davner is an entrepreneur and finance veteran with more than 25 years of experience across capital markets, fund management, and small- and micro-cap finance. Today he serves as Chief Executive Officer of Beverage USA Holdings, Inc. and is co-founder of NERD Focus, a functional energy drink built around nootropics and performance nutrients.

His path into consumer products grew out of years spent evaluating and operating businesses from the capital side. That background shapes how he runs a beverage company: disciplined about unit economics, deliberate about distribution, and patient about brand-building in a crowded, fast-growing category. He holds a Bachelor of Arts in Economics from the University of South Florida.

His latest venture is Provieo (provieo.com), an AI platform he founded that helps students build real, job-tailored projects and get hired on the strength of what they can actually build. It reflects a principle that has guided his whole career: trust demonstrated results over polished claims.

This site is Howard's official home on the web — a place to share his background, the ventures he's building, and his perspective on entrepreneurship, AI, and the functional-beverage market.
