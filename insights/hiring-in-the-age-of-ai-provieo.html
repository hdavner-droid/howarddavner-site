Hiring in the Age of AI: Howard Davner on Why Provieo Matters Now

By Howard Davner · Founder, Provieo · August 2026

Hiring was already broken before AI. Now it's breaking faster. When anyone can generate a flawless résumé and a polished cover letter in seconds, those documents stop telling employers anything. I'm Howard Davner, founder of Provieo, and I think this moment — the collapse of the old hiring signals — is exactly why proof of real work matters more now than at any point in my career.

The signal has collapsed

A hiring manager today opens an inbox full of applications that are all articulate, all keyword-perfect, and all essentially indistinguishable. AI raised the floor on how good an application looks, which means the application no longer separates the capable from the merely well-prompted. When everyone can look qualified, looking qualified is worthless. Employers are left squinting for a real signal in a sea of noise.

Proof cuts through the noise

The one thing AI can't fake on someone's behalf is a body of genuine, explainable work aimed at the job they want. That's the wedge Provieo drives into a broken market. A real project shows how a person actually thinks and executes — and it's checkable, because you can ask them to walk you through it. In a world where claims are free and identical, evidence becomes the scarce and valuable thing.

This is a chance, not just a threat

Most of the conversation about AI and jobs is fearful, and I understand why. But there's an opportunity buried in the disruption. When pedigree-based signals lose their power, the door opens for people who can genuinely do the work but were never going to win the résumé screen. AI's leveling of the surface layer is precisely what makes demonstrated ability the new differentiator. Provieo exists to help people seize that — to compete on what they can build, not on where they've been.

What employers should do now

My advice to the people doing the hiring is simple: stop reading applications as if they still carry information they no longer carry, and start asking to see work. Replace "tell me about your experience" with "show me something you built and walk me through your decisions." The employers who make that shift early will pull ahead, because they'll be reading a real signal while everyone else is still grading polish. That shift is the whole thesis behind Provieo.

Why now

Timing matters for any venture, and I believe Provieo's is right. The problem it solves didn't exist at this scale two years ago; AI created it, and AI keeps making it worse for anyone relying on old signals. Building the infrastructure for proof-based hiring isn't a nice-to-have anymore — it's becoming the only reliable way to match good people with good work. That's the future I'm building toward, and why I think this is the moment for it.
